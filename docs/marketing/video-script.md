# A 45-second video of the app

You record it. One video, two voice-overs: the pictures are the same, so you can record the screen once and lay English or Spanish on top.

**Length:** about 45 seconds (it works as a 30-second cut too: see the bottom).
**Shape:** vertical, 1080 × 1920, from a phone.
**Feel:** calm. Slow taps. Let each screen sit for a beat. No fast cuts, no loud music (or no music at all: a quiet instrumental you have the license for).

## Before you record

- Use a **fresh profile**: a new private browser window, or a phone that has never opened the app. Nothing real on the screen: no real email, name, goal or amounts. The test goal should be something ordinary, like a car.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb so no notification pops up mid-take.
- Show the real app. Don't edit the screens or add fake numbers. If a screen shows something you don't want to say out loud, don't read it out.
- Put captions on (the on-screen text below), because most people watch with the sound off.

Spoken length: roughly 80 words in English and 90 in Spanish. At a calm two words a second, that leaves a little air at the ends.

## The end card

A cream background with the navy logo and wordmark, the tagline in the same serif face as the app, and under it, small: *Educational only. Not financial advice.* / *Solo con fines educativos. No es asesoría financiera.* Hold it for two seconds with nothing moving.

## Words to avoid in the voice-over

Don't say "the best", "the only", "thousands of", "trusted by", "guaranteed", "you'll save" or "you'll earn". They aren't true today, or can't be shown. Don't promise results. Say what the app does.

## A 30-second cut

Keep 0:00–0:04 (the hook), the first-question beat, the goal and lesson beat, the tutor beat, and the promise end card. Drop the language-switch and portfolio beats. Voice-over: the first sentence of each remaining row.

## Checklist before you post

- [ ] Nothing real is on screen (email, name, amounts, notifications).
- [ ] Every screen is the real app, and nothing was edited.
- [ ] The words you say are in the table, or are just as true.
- [ ] Captions are on, and they match the voice.
- [ ] The end card shows the line *Educational only. Not financial advice.*
- [ ] If there's music, you have the license for it.
