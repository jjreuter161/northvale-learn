# Brand check: "Northvale Learn", the sailboat logo, "Find your open water"

A **first check** (2026-11-10), not legal advice. It looks for obvious conflicts in public sources. **A real trademark clearance search, and filing, should be done with a lawyer before a public launch.** Nothing was bought or registered, and nothing is renamed in the code.

**Short version:**
- No app, company or trademark called "Northvale" was found in finance, software or education, in the US or Mexico. That's good enough to keep the name for the tester beta.
- But the short versions are taken: **northvale.com** (parked, for sale) and **@northvale** on Instagram, TikTok and X (all inactive).
- The longer forms are free: **northvalelearn.com**, **northvalelearn.app**, the **.mx** domains, and **@northvalelearn** everywhere.
- ⚠️ marks what a lawyer should look at.

---

## App Store and Google Play

**No finance or education apps named Northvale.**
- Apple's App Store search lists apps from businesses in **Northvale, New Jersey** (a diner, a gym and a liquor store), plus unrelated "North…" names.
- Google Play shows the same local businesses, and a few "North…" apps (NorthWave, Northgate, North Sails).

⚠️ **"Northvale" is also a town (Northvale, NJ).** Search results, store searches and business directories are full of local listings. That's not a legal conflict, but it makes it harder to be found by name. A lawyer can say whether a place name affects registering it.

## The slogan: "Find your open water"

- **No brand found using it,** in web searches or the USPTO search.
- Nearby marks are in other fields: "OPEN WATER" (bottled water, clothing, swimming pools), "OPEN WATER CHAMPIONSHIPS" (events), and a pending **"FIND YOUR WATER"** (clothing).
- The phrase is common among open-water swimmers, which doesn't stop us using it.
- Spanish: "Rumbo a mar abierto" (recommended in `docs/design/brand/PROPOSAL.md`) wasn't checked separately; the lawyer should include it.

## The sailboat logo

Simple sailboat icons are very common, especially in finance and boating. Two notes for the lawyer:
- **North Sails**, a large sailmaker, combines "North" and sails, though in a different field.
- A logo you can protect needs its own design search. Option C (the north star) is the most distinctive of the three.

---

## What I'd do

1. **For the tester beta: keep "Northvale Learn".** Nothing found blocks it.
2. **Your choices (they need your accounts, or money):**
   - claim **@northvalelearn** on Instagram, TikTok and X;
   - consider **northvalelearn.com** (about the price of a normal domain).
   - Buying **northvale.com** (for sale, price unknown) isn't needed now.
3. **Before a public launch:** a lawyer's clearance search in the US and Mexico, then filing for "Northvale Learn" (and the logo) in classes 9 and 41, and maybe 36. Ask them specifically about SÍ VALE and NORTHWARE in Mexico, the place name, and the slogan.
