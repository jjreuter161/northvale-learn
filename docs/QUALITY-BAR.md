# The quality bar: how each item is tested, and where it stands

"Done" means all ten are true, for a brand-new learner, at phone size (390 px) and laptop size (1366 px). This file says how each one is checked, with what, and what a script cannot judge. The scripted checks live in `test/quality.test.mjs` and `test/usability.test.mjs` (both run in `npm test`).

| # | Item | How it is checked | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First minute: open to first answered question in under 60 s, no confusion | `quality.test.mjs` and `browser.test.mjs`: 3 taps, a few seconds, at both sizes, no console errors. "No confusion" is a person's judgement: the audit's welcome and first-lesson fixes (items 1, 3, 11, 15) are what moved it | Passes |
| 2 | Every screen says where you are, what to do, why it matters | `quality.test.mjs`: each main screen (Today, Goals, Practice, Missions, Money Year, Tools, Settings, Achievements, Skills) has a title and a line saying what it is for; this round added the missing lines to Settings, Achievements, Skills and Invite | Passes |
| 3 | Nothing broken on Chrome, Safari and Firefox | Chrome: every test. Safari (WebKit) and Firefox: `test/browsers.test.mjs`, which needs the two engines installed | **Chrome passes. Safari and Firefox not run: the engines are not installed and downloading them needs the owner's OK (Firefox 106 MB, WebKit 79 MB, ffmpeg 1 MB, from cdn.playwright.dev into `~/Library/Caches/ms-playwright`)** |
| 4 | Nothing feels empty | `quality.test.mjs`: every main screen has more than 90 characters of real content and something to read or do, at both sizes | Passes |
| 5 | Speed: nothing over about a second on a normal connection | `quality.test.mjs`, with the network held to 10 Mbps and 40 ms: first screen about 0.45 s, a tab switch 30 to 60 ms, a lesson opening about 0.1 s. Measured earlier on a slow 4G profile (1.6 Mbps, 150 ms): the first screen about 1.9 s | Passes on a normal connection; about 2 s on slow 4G |
| 6 | Lessons feel worth it | Not scriptable. All 63 lessons are checked for length (4.5 minutes or less in both languages) and at least 3 kinds of question. Ten lessons were read in full (budget-1, save-2, banking-1, paycheck-2, credit-1, scams-1, compound-1, stocks-1, etf-1, risk-1): the structure is sound (two short cards, three questions of different kinds, a tool). The four easiest true-or-false questions were rewritten as questions that need thought (budget-1, save-2, etf-1, scams-1), a worked number was added to the 50/30/20 card, and an exclamation mark in the app's own voice was removed | Partly: those ten were read and improved; the other 53 were checked by the validator only. A real learner's view of "worth it" is still to be gathered (see `docs/TESTER-SCRIPT.md`) |
| 7 | Consistency | `quality.test.mjs`: one page-title size on every screen, full-width buttons one height, every control at least 43 px (`usability.test.mjs`) | Passes for what a script can see; spacing between sections is by eye |
| 8 | You can always get back, never stuck | `quality.test.mjs`: every screen below a tab has a Back that goes up one level; `browser.test.mjs`: Back closes sheets and lessons in the browser's own history | Passes in Chrome; real phones unconfirmed |
| 9 | A laptop looks like a desktop app | `quality.test.mjs`: a sidebar, no bottom tab bar, nothing wider than the window, no stretched lines; `layout.test.mjs` at 1366 and 1920 | Passes. One judgement left for the owner: the lesson player is a centered column about 640 px wide on a laptop (a common pattern, but narrow) |
| 10 | Calm and professional | `quality.test.mjs`: no exclamation marks or emoji on any main screen; the lesson content was scanned (the only exclamation marks left are inside quoted scam messages, on purpose) | Passes for words; "nothing childish" is a design judgement |
