# Sailing identity: proposals (nothing is in the app yet)

Name: **Northvale Learn** (unchanged). Tagline: **"Find your open water."** It ties the learning app to the full Northvale finance app later, and it's the brand story: financial freedom is being out on open water.

Nothing here goes into the app, the landing page or the store listings until you approve it, and until the first name check (`docs/BRAND-CHECK.md`) comes back clean enough to go ahead.

---

## 1. The tagline in Spanish

| Option | How it reads | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Rumbo a mar abierto** (recommended) | "Heading for open sea" | The most natural as a slogan. "Rumbo" is a sailing word (a course you set), and it sounds like a direction you choose, not a thing you're told to find. |
| Encuentra tu mar abierto | "Find your open sea" | The closest to the English. Understandable and fine, but "tu mar abierto" sounds a little translated. |
| Hacia aguas abiertas | "Toward open waters" | Avoid: in Spanish, "aguas abiertas" mostly means open-water *swimming*. |

My suggestion: keep **"Find your open water"** in English and use **"Rumbo a mar abierto"** in Spanish. A slogan works best when each language sounds like it was written in that language.

## 2. Three logo options

See `logos.png` (made by `logos.mjs`; the single marks are in `logo-a.svg`, `logo-b.svg`, `logo-c.svg`). Each is one color, so it works printed, engraved, as a small app icon, in navy on cream and in cream on navy.

- **A · Sloop:** two geometric sails over a hull. The clearest at tiny sizes (29 px, the smallest phone icon); classic and calm.
- **B · Open water:** one wind-filled sail drawn as a single line, over a wave. The most modern and the most "open water", but its thin lines get faint at the very smallest sizes.
- **C · North star:** a sail with a small four-point star above it. It's navigating by the north star, the "North" in Northvale, and it echoes today's compass icon.

My recommendation is **C**. It's the only one that tells our story, not just "a boat", and it stays clear at small sizes. A is the safe choice. B is the prettiest at large size.

A note for the name check: simple sailboat icons are common, especially in finance. A mark you can protect needs a lawyer's search, not just a look.

## 3. Nautical touches: a short list (only where they fit)

What I'd add, all small:
1. **The welcome screen:** the tagline under the logo, "Find your open water" / "Rumbo a mar abierto".
2. **The app icon and logo:** the option you pick, replacing today's compass.
3. **Finishing the whole course** (the rare, big moment): "You finished the whole course. Open water ahead." / "Terminaste todo el curso. Hay mar abierto por delante."
4. **The landing page:** the tagline in the hero and the brand story (below) near the end, with the small mark.

What I'd keep out: anchors, ship wheels, ropes or knots as decoration, wave backgrounds, pirate talk ("ahoy", "matey"), cartoon boats or sea creatures. No sailing jokes in errors, scams, debt or money-stress content. The finish screen, streaks and moments stay calm and plain; the voice guide (Milestone 4.5, Step 4) carries the personality, not the theme.

## 4. The brand story (landing page and the "Our promise" page)

**English**

> Out on open water, you choose where you go. Nobody else sets your course, and that's about as free as a person gets. But you can't sail far until your money is under control: knowing where it goes, keeping some of it, and making it grow. That's what Northvale is for. Find your open water.

**Español**

> En mar abierto, tú decides a dónde vas. Nadie más marca tu rumbo, y eso es lo más libre que puede ser una persona. Pero no se llega lejos hasta que tu dinero está bajo control: saber a dónde va, guardar una parte y hacerlo crecer. Para eso es Northvale. Rumbo a mar abierto.

(The "Our promise" page is part of Milestone 5; the story goes on it when that page is built.)
