SimpleExpert
Why keep an emergency fund in a savings account?
Because you might need it fast. A savings account keeps it apart from your spending money, you can get to it in a day or two, and in the US it's usually insured by the FDIC up to $250,000 per bank. Investing it could mean selling at a bad time.
Covered in Saving · Where to keep savings
what about for teens?
Same idea. Under 18, you usually open it with a parent or guardian (a joint or custodial account). Look for no monthly fee and no minimum balance.
Ask anything about money…

For learning, not advice. It never tells you what to buy.
