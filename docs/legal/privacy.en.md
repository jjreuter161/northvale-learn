# Privacy Policy

**DRAFT — for the beta. A lawyer must review this before a public launch.** Last updated: November 2026.

Northvale Learn teaches money and investing with pretend money. We built it to collect as little about you as possible. This page says exactly what we keep, why, and how to delete it.

## The short version

- You can use Northvale Learn without an account. Your progress then stays on your device.
- We never ask for your real name, birthday, address, phone number or bank details.
- We don't show ads, we don't sell or share your data, and there are no third-party trackers.
- You can delete your account and your data at any time: Settings → Privacy → Delete my data.

## The waitlist

If you join the waitlist on our website (/welcome), we keep only what the form asks: your email, your country, whether you're a student, teacher, parent or other, and your age range. For ages 13–17, we also keep the date you confirmed a parent or guardian said it's OK (people under 13 can't join). We also keep the language of the page you used and the date you joined.

- **Why:** only to tell you about the beta (and, for teachers, when classroom mode is ready). No newsletters or marketing from anyone else.
- **Email:** once our email is set up, you get one short confirmation (sent through Google's Gmail), with a link to take yourself off the list. We'll write again when there's a spot for you.
- **The website has no trackers,** no outside fonts and no cookies. To stop spam, it has a hidden field that only bots fill in, and the server limits how often one network address can sign up (the address is used for that briefly and not stored with your entry).
- **Taking yourself off:** use the link in the email (or on the "You're on the list" screen), or write to us. Your entry is deleted right away. Otherwise we delete the list within 30 days after the beta ends.

## What stays on your device

Your lessons (including where you are in one you haven't finished), XP, streak, badges, practice portfolio, goals, real-life missions (and any notes you write for them), your answers to the daily myth, your age range if you choose one, tutor chat, the classes you're in and your invite link are saved in your browser on this device. We can't see them unless you create an account (or join a class, below).

**Your look** (Cream, Navy or Match my device) is a setting on this device. With Match my device, the app checks whether your device is in dark mode; that's never sent anywhere. Lessons you've opened are also kept on the device so they work offline.

**Share cards** (images like "12-day streak") are made on your device. They never show names, location or your class, and they show a goal's name or amounts only if you turn that on for that one card (not possible for ages 13–17). We only count that a card of a certain kind (streak, unit, badge, goal) was shared or saved.

## What the server receives

**If you use the app without an account:**

- **Usage stats.** We get a random ID made on your device (not tied to you), plus what happened in the app, such as "finished a lesson", "reached step 3 of a lesson" or "finished a mission" (never what you wrote in a mission note), your language and country setting, and the date. We use this only to learn whether Northvale Learn helps people and where it's confusing. You can turn it off in Settings → Privacy. It's off automatically if your browser sends "Global Privacy Control" or "Do Not Track".
- **Feedback and survey answers** you choose to send.
- **Error reports** when something breaks: the error message and where in the app it happened. These contain no personal information.
- **Requests for news and prices.** These don't include anything about you.
- **Daily reminders, only if you turn them on.** The address your browser's push service gives this device, the time you picked, your time zone and language, and a few words about what's next (your streak, and the titles of your next lesson and this week's mission), so the reminder can say something useful. They're kept under a random ID made on your device, not linked to your account or usage stats, and deleted when you turn reminders off or choose "Delete my data".
- **A class, only if you join one** (a test feature that's on only for invited teachers). No account or email is needed. We keep the class you're in, the nickname you pick for it, your age range (to check you're 13 or older) and, from the day you join: which lessons you finish, how many real-life missions you finish (never your notes), how many questions you answered and missed on each topic, and this week's XP. **Your teacher sees** your nickname, the lessons and units you've finished since joining, how many missions you've finished, and your progress on what they assign. Topic results reach the teacher only as totals for the whole class, once 3 or more students have answered. **Your teacher never sees** your goals, amounts, practice portfolio, mission notes, tutor questions, age or email, or anything from before you joined (unless you choose to count lessons you'd already finished toward an assignment). Classmates see your nickname and weekly XP on the class leaderboard, unless you hide yourself. There are no messages of any kind.
- **Invite links, only if you make or open one.** Your link is a random code. When someone opens it and finishes a first lesson, we count it under a random ID made just for that (not their usage-stats ID or account), so both of you get a streak freeze. We never read your contacts or send anything for you.

**If you create an account, we also keep:**

- your email (to sign in, and to send you a password reset link if you ask for one) and your password, stored only in a scrambled form that can't be turned back into the password;
- your nickname, avatar color and age range (for example "13–17");
- for ages 13–17, the date you confirmed a parent or guardian agreed;
- your synced progress, so it's the same on every device, with a few words about what's next (your goals' progress, the next lesson, this week's mission);
- whether you turned on the weekly email recap. If you do, it's made from your synced progress and sent to your email on Sunday evenings; every email has a link to turn it off;
- your weekly XP, so you can be in a league (other learners see only your nickname and XP; ages 13–17 are only in leagues with other 13–17s), and who you compete with: everyone in this week's group, only friends, or only your class;
- friends you added through an invite link: each of you sees the other's nickname and weekly XP, nothing else, and either can remove the link;
- if you confirm your email (needed to teach a class), the date you did; if you teach, the date you accepted the teacher terms and your classes (their names, codes and assignments).
- how many tutor questions you asked today (to keep the service fair).

## The AI tutor and AI news summaries

When you ask the tutor a question, your question is sent to Anthropic, the company that makes the AI (Claude), so it can be answered. It goes along with a few broad facts: your level, the units you finished, your age range, and what's in your practice portfolio. We don't send your email or nickname, and our server doesn't keep your questions. Anthropic handles this data under its commercial terms. **[Lawyer to confirm: retention period, and that inputs aren't used for training.]**

## Your IP address

The server uses your IP address for a few minutes to stop password-guessing and spam. We don't store it with your data. Our hosting provider may keep it in short-term technical logs.

## Who else processes data

- **Our hosting provider** **Fly.io, with servers in Dallas, Texas (US)** runs the server and stores the data.
- **Anthropic** answers tutor questions and writes the news summaries.
- **Twelve Data** sends us market prices. It never receives anything about you.
- **Google (Gmail)** delivers password reset emails and, if you turn it on, the weekly recap. It receives your email address and the email. We never send newsletters or marketing.
- **Your browser's push service** (Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft, depending on your browser) delivers daily reminders if you turn them on. Each reminder is encrypted for your device, so the push service can't read it.

## How long we keep it

- **Account data** stays until you delete the account. Deleted data can stay in our backups for up to 14 days (and, if we ever restore a backup, in the copy of the data it replaced, which we also delete after 14 days).
- **Usage stats** are kept as small counts and deleted when you choose "Delete my data".
- **Feedback** is kept for up to 2 years. After "Delete my data" it's no longer linked to your device.
- **Class data** stays until you leave the class, your teacher removes you, or the class is deleted. A class the teacher archives is deleted 90 days later, and deleting a teacher's account deletes their classes. "Delete my data" takes you out of every class.
- **Invite links** stay until you choose "Delete my data" (or delete your account, which removes your friend links).
- If the beta ends, we delete everything within 30 days.

## Children and teens

Northvale Learn is for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have, we delete it. **[Contact email]**

People aged 13–17 need a parent's or guardian's permission to create an account.

Classes are for students 13 and older. Joining one needs no account or email: only a nickname and an age range. Teachers must be adults with a confirmed email who accept the teacher terms, which ask them to get their school's OK first.

## Your rights

You can see, correct, download, delete or object to the use of your data. In Mexico these are your ARCO rights. In some US states you have similar rights. Most of this you can do in the app (Settings → Privacy). For anything else, write to **[contact email]** and we'll answer within 20 business days.
