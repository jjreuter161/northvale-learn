# Privacy Policy

**DRAFT — for the beta. A lawyer must review this before a public launch.** Last updated: October 2026.

Northvale Learn teaches money and investing with pretend money. We built it to collect as little about you as possible. This page says exactly what we keep, why, and how to delete it.

## The short version

- You can use Northvale Learn without an account. Your progress then stays on your device.
- We never ask for your real name, birthday, address, phone number or bank details.
- We don't show ads, we don't sell or share your data, and there are no third-party trackers.
- You can delete your account and your data at any time: Settings → Privacy → Delete my data.

## What stays on your device

Your lessons, XP, streak, badges, practice portfolio, goals, Money Missions (and any notes you write for them), your age range if you choose one, and tutor chat are saved in your browser on this device. We can't see them unless you create an account.

## What the server receives

**If you use the app without an account:**

- **Usage stats.** We get a random ID made on your device (not tied to you), plus what happened in the app, such as "finished a lesson", "reached step 3 of a lesson" or "finished a mission" (never what you wrote in a mission note), your language and country setting, and the date. We use this only to learn whether Northvale Learn helps people and where it's confusing. You can turn it off in Settings → Privacy. It's off automatically if your browser sends "Global Privacy Control" or "Do Not Track".
- **Feedback and survey answers** you choose to send.
- **Error reports** when something breaks: the error message and where in the app it happened. These contain no personal information.
- **Requests for news and prices.** These don't include anything about you.

**If you create an account, we also keep:**

- your email (to sign in, and to send you a password reset link if you ask for one) and your password, stored only in a scrambled form that can't be turned back into the password;
- your nickname, avatar color and age range (for example "13–17");
- for ages 13–17, the date you confirmed a parent or guardian agreed;
- your synced progress, so it's the same on every device;
- your weekly XP, so you can be in a league (other learners see only your nickname and XP);
- how many tutor questions you asked today (to keep the service fair).

## The AI tutor and AI news summaries

When you ask the tutor a question, your question is sent to Anthropic, the company that makes the AI (Claude), so it can be answered. It goes along with a few broad facts: your level, the units you finished, your age range, and what's in your practice portfolio. We don't send your email or nickname, and our server doesn't keep your questions. Anthropic handles this data under its commercial terms. **[Lawyer to confirm: retention period, and that inputs aren't used for training.]**

## Your IP address

The server uses your IP address for a few minutes to stop password-guessing and spam. We don't store it with your data. Our hosting provider may keep it in short-term technical logs.

## Who else processes data

- **Our hosting provider** **[name, e.g. Fly.io, and the region]** runs the server and stores the data.
- **Anthropic** answers tutor questions and writes the news summaries.
- **Twelve Data** sends us market prices. It never receives anything about you.
- **Google (Gmail)** delivers password reset emails, and only those. It receives your email address and the reset link. We never send newsletters or marketing.

## How long we keep it

- **Account data** stays until you delete the account. Deleted data can stay in our backups for up to 14 days.
- **Usage stats** are kept as small counts and deleted when you choose "Delete my data".
- **Feedback** is kept for up to 2 years. After "Delete my data" it's no longer linked to your device.
- If the beta ends, we delete everything within 30 days.

## Children and teens

Northvale Learn is for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have, we delete it. **[Contact email]**

People aged 13–17 need a parent's or guardian's permission to create an account.

## Your rights

You can see, correct, download, delete or object to the use of your data. In Mexico these are your ARCO rights. In some US states you have similar rights. Most of this you can do in the app (Settings → Privacy). For anything else, write to **[contact email]** and we'll answer within 20 business days.
