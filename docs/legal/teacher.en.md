# Teacher Terms

**DRAFT — classroom mode is a test feature. A lawyer must review these terms, and the student-privacy points in docs/COMPLIANCE.md, before any school uses it with real students.** Last updated: October 2026.

These terms are for adults who create a class in Northvale Learn. They apply on top of the regular Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy.

## 1. Who can teach

- You must be **18 or older** and use an account with an email address you've confirmed.
- You're a teacher, a club leader or a youth program leader, and you use classes to teach money basics.

## 2. Before you use it with students

- **Get your school's or organization's OK first.** Follow its rules for apps students use (many schools keep a list of approved apps or need a signed agreement). If your school needs a written agreement with us, contact us before you start.
- **Tell students' families,** the way your school usually does for a new app. The printable teacher guide has a short note you can send.
- **Classes are for students 13 and older** for now.

## 3. Student privacy — what you agree to

- **Nicknames only.** Let students choose a nickname. Don't require their real or full names in the app.
- **Don't collect personal information** through Northvale Learn: no emails, phone numbers, addresses, birthdays or photos. The app doesn't ask for them in a class, and you shouldn't ask either.
- **Use what you see only for teaching.** You see nicknames, lessons and units finished since each student joined, how many real-life missions they finished, progress on what you assign, and class-wide totals for hard topics. Don't copy it anywhere else except your own grade book, and only when your school allows that.
- **Never share it with anyone outside your school** or use it for anything but teaching that class.
- **Students can leave any time.** Leaving removes them from your view. Don't pressure them to rejoin or to stay on the leaderboard.

## 4. What Northvale Learn does

- Shows you only the information listed above. **You never see** students' goals, amounts, practice portfolios, mission notes, tutor questions, age or email.
- Has **no messaging** of any kind: not between students, and not between you and students.
- Never sells student information, never uses it for ads, and never builds profiles of students for anything but their own learning.
- Deletes a student's class data when they leave, when you remove them, or when the class is deleted. **An archived class is deleted 90 days after you archive it.** Deleting your account deletes your classes.

## 5. It's a test

Classroom mode is new and may change, pause or stop. All money in Northvale Learn is pretend, and nothing in it is financial advice.

## 6. Contact

Questions, or a school that needs a signed agreement: write to the contact email in the Privacy Policy.
