# Our promise

Out on open water, you choose where you go. Nobody else sets your course, and that's about as free as a person gets. But you can't sail far until your money is under control: knowing where it goes, keeping some of it, and making it grow. That's what Northvale is for. Find your open water.

## What we promise

- **No ads.** Nothing in Northvale Learn is an ad, and nobody pays to be shown to you.
- **We never sell your data.** We don't sell or share it, and there are no third-party trackers. The privacy policy says exactly what we keep and why.
- **Nobody pays us to recommend anything.** No bank, broker or company pays Northvale Learn to recommend a product. The investments in the practice portfolio are well-known examples to learn with, not recommendations.
- **Lessons teach, they don't sell.** We explain how money works. We never tell you what to buy or sell.
- **Pretend money only.** No real money moves in the app, and no bank account is connected.
