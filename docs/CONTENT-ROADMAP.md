# Content roadmap: from 18 units and 63 lessons to 50 units and 225 lessons

**Status: a plan for your approval. Nothing here is built yet.** When you approve (or change) the order, Wave 1 starts.

## In one page

- **Today:** 18 units, 63 lessons (nine units have 3 lessons, nine have 4).
- **Goal:** 50 units, 225 lessons: the 18 we have (nine of them grow from 3 to 5 lessons, +18 lessons) and **32 new units** (16 with 5 lessons, 16 with 4: 144 lessons). 63 + 18 + 144 = **225**.
- **Twelve tracks, three levels.** Tracks group units by what a person is trying to do; levels (**Start, Build, Go further**) say how far in they are. A learner sees the track their goal and country point to first.
- **The biggest strength comes first.** Wave 1 is the border: sending money, banking in two countries, working across the border, taxes in two countries, border scams, plus digital payments, credit scores and judging a job offer.
- **Four waves of eight new units**, each with a human finance review before it goes live. About 36 new lessons per wave, plus a few added to existing units.
- **Same standards as today:** every lesson 3–4.5 minutes, at least 3 kinds of question, an "In real life" line, a real-life mission per unit, every changeable number in `facts.js` and `docs/VERIFY-BEFORE-LAUNCH.md`, both languages, US and Mexico versions where the system differs, never advice.

## The full outline

For each unit: **After this unit you can…**; the lessons (English titles here; Spanish titles are written with each wave); the **versions** it needs (**Both** = a US and a Mexico version of every lesson, **Border** = always written for both countries at once); a **mission** idea (a small real-life task, like today's 32); and the **landmark** for the sea chart. ## The sea chart and the Learn tab with 50 units

**The problem:** one long chart of 50 islands is too much on a phone, and "show 4 more units" doesn't help people find their way.

**The idea: archipelagos.** Each track is a small archipelago (a region of the chart); each level is a band of water: **Start** (the harbor), **Build** (the coast), **Go further** (open water). Islands are the units.

- **On a phone:** Learn opens on your **current track** (a chart of 3 to 10 islands, the way it is today). A row of **track chips** sits above it (each with a small progress ring and "3 of 6"), scrolling sideways. Tap a chip to move to that archipelago; the one your goal and country point to has a small "For you" tag. A "Whole map" chip shows all twelve regions as one overview with progress.
- **On a computer:** a **track rail** on the left of Learn (the twelve tracks with rings and counts), the **region's chart** in the middle (bigger, with the level bands labeled), and the panel on the right (today's step, "11 of 50 units").
- **Finished tracks fold up** into a single flag on the whole map; unfinished ones stay open.
- **Order stays gentle:** units inside a track unlock in order within a level; a new level opens when the one before it is mostly done (or after a short "skip ahead" check, as today).
- **Where it goes by default:** the track of your goal if you have one, else Foundations for new learners, else the track you were last in.

## Quality plan

### Templates
- A **unit template** (`docs/templates/unit.md` and a starter file) lists everything a unit needs before writing begins: id, title (EN/ES), track, level, outcome, countries (US, Mexico or Border), concepts (each with `explain`, `easy` and `challenge`), lessons (2 cards, 3 questions of at least 3 kinds, an action, an "In real life" line), a mission, the facts it uses, its sources, its landmark.
- A **lesson template** with the word counts the validator measures (cards 40–70 words), the question kinds, the way to write `miss` for each wrong choice, and the Spanish and Mexico-version rules from `docs/CONTENT-STYLE.md`.

### The validator (`scripts/validate-content.mjs`), today and new
Today it checks translations, concept tags, explanations, facts, actions and length (3–4.5 minutes). New rules for the bigger course:
1. Every unit names **at least two official sources** (one per country where the system differs), with a URL and the date they were last checked.
2. Every `{@fact}` has a row in `docs/FACTS.md` with its source and a **"verified on"** date; any older than 12 months fails the build.
3. A unit can't ship without a **reviewer sign-off** (see below): the unit file carries `reviewed: "2027-01-15 · initials"`; unreviewed units are hidden in production.
4. **Both-country completeness:** every `T()` that differs by country has both the US and Mexico text in both languages.
5. **Advice-word scan** ("should", "best", "always", "guaranteed", "will" in teaching text) with an allow-list for quoted examples.
6. **Numbers in both currencies** are checked by a script that recomputes the worked examples.
7. **A mission** exists for every unit, starts with a verb, never asks for personal numbers, and has an adult-only or teen version where an account or credit history is involved.
8. **Reading level** (about grade 7–8 in English; sentence length in both languages).
9. **Variety:** at least 3 question kinds per lesson, and no unit uses one kind more than half the time.

### Review checklist (every lesson, before a wave goes to a human)
1. Validator passes. 2. Read aloud in both languages; cut anything you stumble on. 3. Every number checked in both countries and both currencies. 4. No advice words; risky things described accurately, including how they can lose everything. 5. Every claim traces to a source in the unit's source list. 6. Played in the app at phone size and laptop size. 7. The unit's mission was done by a real person once.
