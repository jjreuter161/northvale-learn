# Northvale Learn

Set a goal, get a plan, and learn what it takes to reach it, five minutes a day. It's educational only: no real money, no real trades, no bank connections.

- **Goals first.** Home is your goal dashboard.
  - Pick a goal (emergency fund, car, college, first investment, travel, paying off debt, or your own) with country-appropriate starting amounts. You get a dated monthly plan on a path: cash, savings account / CETES, or index fund.
  - Each goal shows what you've saved so far vs. the target, time left, and whether you're on track, behind or ahead of the plan.
  - Practice investments linked to a goal show on their own line ("with practice investments"), so you can see how investing could get you there sooner.
  - You record money you set aside, and entries stay visible after edits.
  - Milestones at 25/50/75/100% are marked with a calm moment and an achievement.
  - A Sunday check-in sums up the week.
  - Each goal keeps its own currency, with an approximate conversion if you switch country.
  - You can have several goals and edit, pause or delete them.
- **Lessons.** 17 units with 59 lessons of 3–5 minutes each. They run from budgeting, saving, banking, your first paycheck, credit and scams through compound interest, taxes, insurance, the psychology of money, stocks, funds, risk, crypto and risky bets, retirement and earning more, to reading financial news. Each lesson has short cards, quick questions with instant feedback, and ends with a real action in the practice portfolio or a goal tool (including paycheck, sales tax/IVA, tax brackets, bank fees, scam red flags, insurance, volatility and side-hustle tools). The next lesson is picked for your goal, and each finished lesson says how it helps that goal.
- **A learning engine that remembers.**
  - Every question tests a concept (59 of them), and each learner's mastery of each concept is tracked: new → learning → solid → mastered.
  - A 2–3 minute **Review** brings back concepts you missed or haven't seen in a while, using spaced repetition. It counts toward the daily goal and the streak.
  - A wrong answer says why that choice is wrong, then gives the answer. In a lesson, a miss also brings another explanation and an easier question right away.
  - A perfect lesson unlocks a harder **Challenge** version.
  - Me shows a skills overview.
- **Money Missions.** Small real-life tasks that put a lesson to work: "Track everything you spend for 3 days", "Find the fees on a bank account", "Read a real pay stub", "Spot 3 ads that use FOMO", "Set aside any amount toward your goal". There are 31, one suggested each week on Home. They're picked for your country, age range (some are 18+, and some have a 13–17 version that involves a parent) and goal, and for what you studied this week. A finished lesson suggests its unit's mission. You do them on your own, mark them done and can add a short private note. They earn 20 XP the first time (weekly ones once a week) and three achievements, but don't count for the learning streak. No money moves and nothing is collected; usage stats count only that a mission was finished.
- **First minutes.** Pick a reason, set your first goal (amount and date, prefilled for your country), optionally take a 2-minute placement check that skips units you already know, pick a daily time, and you're in the lesson that helps that goal most. Signing up is offered only after that lesson.
- **Bringing people back.** Everything here is calm and opt-in:
  - **Daily reminders** (notifications from the installed app). The learner picks the time; quiet hours (none 9 pm–8 am) are on by default; at most one a day, and only when something's waiting: a streak at risk, the Sunday check-in, a review, this week's mission or the next lesson. Offered once on Home after a second day of learning, and in Settings. Where a browser can't do it (an iPhone before "Add to Home Screen"), it says how, and the calendar reminder still works.
  - **Welcome back.** After 3+ days away, a short catch-up (your goal, the next lesson, one button to restart) instead of a broken-streak message.
  - **Streak repair.** Once a month, a streak of 2+ days that ended can be brought back for free, within 7 days.
  - **Weekly email recap** (accounts, opt-in, once email is set up): Sunday evening, your goals, the week's lessons and the next step. It stops after 4 weeks without a lesson, and one link turns it off.
  - The admin page shows how each one does: how many people see or use it, and how many come back within 7 days after. It also shows day-30 return and has a "send a test reminder" button.
- **Growth and schools.** Built around privacy for teens:
  - **Invite a friend** (Me): a personal link the learner shares however they like (copy, or the phone's share sheet). When the friend finishes a first lesson, both get a streak freeze. No contacts are read and nothing is sent for anyone.
  - **Share cards:** a clean image of a streak, a finished unit, an achievement or a goal's progress, through the phone's share sheet or saved. No names, location or class on any card; a goal's name and amounts only if an adult turns that on for that one card (13–17 can't).
  - **Compete with** everyone (this week's group, with 13–17 and 18+ kept apart), friends (linked through invite links) or your class. Friends or class takes you out of the everyone league. No public profiles.
  - **Classroom mode** (a test feature, **off** except for accounts turned on from the admin page): a teacher (18+, confirmed email, teacher terms) creates a class, shares a code or link, assigns units with due dates, and sees nicknames, lessons and units finished since joining, missions finished, assignment progress and the class's hardest topics as class totals. Students join without an account, after a screen that says exactly what the teacher sees and doesn't, can hide from the class leaderboard and leave any time. No messages. Archived classes are deleted after 90 days. A printable teacher guide in English and Spanish is at `/teacher-guide.html` and `/guia-docente.html`.
  - The admin page shows invites (made, opened, joined, freezes earned), friend links, shares by kind, classes and students, and the feature switches.
- **Habit.** XP, levels, a daily streak with automatic streak freezes, a daily XP target, achievements and a weekly league (real people only), kept quiet and in the background. The top bar shows only the streak; XP lives on Me. Achievements come from learning and from goal milestones, which never give XP (savings are self-reported). There's also a calendar reminder.
- **Practice portfolio.** $10,000 (or $200,000 MXN) of pretend money. Safe investments, funds and single stocks unlock as you learn. Holdings linked to a goal are tagged with it. Prices are real and delayed with a market data key; otherwise they're samples, clearly labeled.
- **Calculators.** Budget planner, debt payoff, starting early, investing range, retirement, paycheck and side hustle, on Home and as lesson steps.
- **AI tutor.** It explains any term, chart or headline in Simple or Expert mode, always as education, and can relate answers to your goal (it sees the goal's kind and numbers, never the name you typed). Without AI, it answers from a built-in glossary.
- **News, explained.** Real headlines, each with a plain-words "what this means".
- **Languages and countries.** English and Spanish, for the United States and Mexico.
- **Looks like a finance app.** Deep navy, one green accent for progress, neutral grays, Inter with a serif wordmark, tabular figures for money, short fades with no bounce. Light and Dark looks. Before/after screenshots are in [docs/design/](docs/design).
- **A landing page and waitlist** at `/welcome` (Spanish: `/bienvenida`): what Northvale Learn is, who it's for, how it works, real screenshots from the app, a teachers section (classroom mode "coming soon", no date), and a "Join the beta" waitlist (email, country, role, age range; 13+; 13–17 confirm a parent's or guardian's OK). No trackers, outside fonts or made-up claims; share previews in both languages; about 140 KB to first view. The admin page lists, exports (CSV) and deletes sign-ups. Run `node scripts/landing-shots.mjs` after the app's look changes to retake the screenshots.
- **Built for a beta.** Privacy-friendly analytics, a private admin page, "Send feedback" everywhere, a one-question survey after lesson 3, error alerts, daily backups, and an installable phone app that works offline.

## Run it

```bash
npm install
npm run dev        # http://localhost:8950
npm test
```

**Going live:** follow **[docs/LAUNCH.md](docs/LAUNCH.md)**. `npm run preflight` checks everything before launch and prints a pass/fail list: the settings (never their values), Haiku and the AI cap, classroom mode off, every test (browser too), git clean with no keys or data in it, and the legal drafts' contact details. Add `-- --quick` to skip the tests.

**Demo data and the preview:**

- `npm run seed:demo` makes the demo people on a running local server (default `http://localhost:8950`; `-- --url …` for another port): Dani (the demo learner, with goals, three weeks of lessons, a streak and a practice fund) and this week's league (Maya, Leo and Ari; Sofía and Teo in the 13–17 league). Running it again resets them. It refuses any other address, a server that says it's production, and a machine set up as the live one. Sign in as `dani@example.test` (the test password is in `scripts/seed-demo.mjs`).
- `PREVIEW_DIR=/path npm run preview:sync` builds the app and copies it into a preview's own folder (for a preview that can't read this one), keeping that copy's data. It makes the demo people too if they're missing, so an emptied preview folder comes back with one command. Restart the preview server afterwards.

Tests and the demo scripts set `NORTHVALE_DOTENV=0`, so the servers they start never read your real `.env`.

Everything works without keys. To turn on the AI tutor, real prices and reset emails, get the keys (see **[docs/SETUP-KEYS.md](docs/SETUP-KEYS.md)**), then run `npm run setup` and `npm run check-setup`. Keys go only in `.env`, which is never committed and never sent to the app.

| Setting (`.env`) | What it does |
|---|---|
| `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` | AI tutor + news summaries. |
| `TUTOR_MODEL`, `NEWS_MODEL` | Which Claude model to use: `claude-haiku-5-5` (default for the beta; $25 covers ~40,000 answers), `claude-sonnet-5-5` or `claude-opus-5-5`. |
| `AI_MONTHLY_BUDGET_USD` | AI spending stops for the month at this amount (default $25). Alerts are sent at 80% and 100%. |
| `TUTOR_DAILY_LIMIT`, `TUTOR_PER_MINUTE` | Tutor questions allowed per account (defaults: 30 a day, 5 a minute). |
| `TWELVEDATA_API_KEY` | Real delayed prices (free key). Without it, prices are labeled samples. |
| `CETES_RATE`, `CETES_RATE_DATE` | The rate for simulated CETES; otherwise an example rate is used and labeled as one. |
| `ADMIN_TOKEN` | 20+ characters; opens the private stats page at `/admin`. |
| `ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL` | A Discord or Slack webhook for new errors, budget alerts and failed backups. |
| `SIGNUP_CODE` | Accounts need this code (for a closed beta). |
| `SMTP_USER`, `SMTP_PASS`, `PUBLIC_URL` | Password reset emails and the weekly recap, sent from a dedicated Gmail with an app password (docs/SETUP-KEYS.md §4). Reminders need no key: the server makes its own (VAPID) on first start. |
| `CONTACT_EMAIL` | Shown to testers who need help, and in the legal pages. Also how browsers' push services can reach you about reminders (or `PUBLIC_URL`). |
| `MAIL_POSTAL_ADDRESS` | A mailing address for the weekly recap's footer (a P.O. box or virtual mailbox, not a home address). Leave empty until the public launch (docs/COMPLIANCE.md). |
| `PORT`, `HOST`, `ALLOWED_HOSTS`, `TRUST_PROXY`, `NORTHVALE_DATA` | Server and hosting settings. |
| `NORTHVALE_DOTENV` | `0` makes the server ignore `.env` (the tests and demo servers use it). |

## How it's built: a core plus modules

```
src/core/       accounts and sync, profile, XP/streak engine (progress.js),
                facts (facts.js), analytics, errors, settings, English/Spanish,
                design system, legal pages, reminders, share cards
                (share.jsx) and invite links (invites.jsx)
src/modules/
  lessons/      the path, the player (lessons, reviews, challenges), the
                course rules (content.js), one file per unit with its
                lessons and concepts (units/), mastery and spaced review
                (mastery.js), placement and the skills card (practice.jsx)
  portfolio/    practice portfolio, prices, its lesson actions
  goals/        Home: goals, plans, milestones, check-in (plan.js = the
                tested model), the calculators (tools.jsx), its lesson
                actions and its onboarding step
  missions/     Money Missions: the catalog (catalog.js), who they fit and
                the weekly pick (pick.js = the tested rules), Home's card,
                the Missions screen and the card after a lesson
  tutor/        AI tutor + offline glossary
  news/         news, explained
  feedback/     "Send feedback" and the survey
  classroom/    classroom mode (behind a feature switch): joining with a
                code, what a student's device reports (report.js), the
                teacher's class view
server/         accounts, progress sync, leagues, tutor (with budget),
                news, prices, insights (analytics/feedback/errors), alerts,
                classes, invites/friends, feature switches (flags.js),
                backups
web/            the page, offline service worker, icons, fonts, admin page
scripts/        validate-content.mjs — checks every lesson, mission and piece
                of app text (npm test runs it);
                tutor-check.mjs — safety check against the real AI (costs money)
```

The core never imports a module. Each module calls `registerModule({ id, nav, Screen, Overlay, Onboard, actions, facts, services })` (`nav.show({ session })` can hide a menu item, as classroom mode does) and gets its own slice of saved state. `Onboard` is an optional first-run step (Goals uses it for the first goal). Modules don't touch each other's state; they talk through events (`emit`/`on`) or named services (`service("tutor", "open")`). A lesson's final step names an action, such as `"portfolio.buy"`, and whichever module provides it renders it.

### Future modules (not built; this is where they plug in)

A **real bank connection** (Plaid in the US, or a Mexican equivalent), **real investing through a licensed partner broker**, and **real banking through a partner bank** each become a module:

1. Create `src/modules/<name>/` with its own screen, saved-state slice and strings.
2. Import it in `src/main.jsx`.
3. Put its server routes in their own file and add them to the routes table in `server/index.js`.

The core and the other modules don't change. Goals are already built for this: a goal's progress is "what's set aside + what's linked", so a real-accounts module can supply real balances through a service the same way the practice portfolio does today (`service("portfolio", "usePositions")`). The existing **Northvale money manager** (`~/Downloads/northvale`: bank connections and the planning engine) is meant to plug in the same way. Real-money modules will also need their own security review, encryption for stored bank tokens, and licensed partners. None of that is in this version.

## Privacy and safety

- **Accounts are optional.** Without one, progress stays on the device.
- **What an account stores:** email, nickname, age range (13+ only; 13–17 with a parent's or guardian's OK) and progress.
- **Analytics:**
  - first-party only, with a random install ID; no name, email, IP or trackers;
  - can be switched off, and are off automatically with Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.
- **Reminders** are tied to a random ID made on the device (not the account or the usage-stats ID). The server keeps the browser's push address, the time, time zone and language, and a few words about what's next (streak, next lesson, mission titles). It's deleted when reminders are turned off or with Delete my data. Push addresses are accepted only from browsers' push services.
- **The weekly recap** is off unless the learner turns it on; it's built from their synced progress. After 4 weeks without a lesson it pauses itself, with one short note.
- **Classes** (testers only for now): students join with a nickname and an age range, no account or email. Their device sends only what the teacher may see (lessons since joining, a mission count, answers per topic, weekly XP). Leaving, or Delete my data, deletes it.
- **Invites and share cards:** no contacts read, nothing sent for you; share cards are made on the device and only their kind is counted.
- **Mission notes** are private: saved with your progress (synced only if you have an account), never sent with usage stats. The note box asks people to leave out account numbers and passwords.
- **Fonts are self-hosted,** so opening the app (or the landing page) contacts no third party.
- **The waitlist** keeps only email, country, role and age range (plus the language, the date and, for 13–17, when they confirmed a parent's OK); each entry can take itself off with its link.
- **Delete my data** (Settings → Privacy) removes the account, this device's usage stats and the device's data.
- **Educational only:**
  - a notice on every money screen;
  - the tutor never recommends buying or selling anything;
  - sample prices and rates are always labeled.

See COMPLIANCE.md and SECURITY-REVIEW.md for details.

## Tests

**Click-through tests in a real browser** (`test/browser.test.mjs`, last in `npm test`, about 15 seconds). Headless Chrome uses the app the way a learner does, on a throwaway server: onboarding, a full lesson, the age question, a Money Mission with a note, a new goal, every calculator, a review, a practice buy, every lesson's "do it" tool, and every screen in English (US) and Spanish (Mexico). It also runs classroom mode end to end (a teacher turned on from the admin page confirms their email, accepts the terms and makes a class; a student joins after the "what your teacher sees" screen, does an assigned lesson, hides from the leaderboard and leaves; the teacher's screen and data are checked for every private detail), share cards (no goal name or amounts by default; no option at all for 13–17), an invite link's streak freezes, and the landing page and waitlist in both languages (every picture loads, nothing from other sites, under 13 blocked, 13–17 with a parent's or guardian's OK, on and off the list, also from the email's link). Any screen it passes fails the test if it shows a raw text key (like `compare.go`), an unfilled `{placeholder}`, `NaN` or `undefined`, or logs a console error. It uses the installed Google Chrome; on a machine without it, run `npx playwright-core install chromium` once. `npm run test:browser` runs only this part.

`npm test` runs `test/engines.test.mjs`, `test/content.test.mjs` (the content validator: both languages and countries, concept tags, explanations, facts, actions, 3–5 minutes, missions, and no missing or duplicated app text), `test/mastery.test.mjs` (mastery levels, spacing, review picking, the migration for existing learners, placement), `test/missions.test.mjs` (which missions fit, their order, the weekly suggestion, notes, XP once, achievements), `test/goals.test.mjs` (plans; on track / behind / ahead; what it takes from here; saved vs. "with practice investments"; milestones once each; re-planning that keeps entries; the Sunday check-in; per-goal currency and conversion; templates), `test/mail.test.mjs` (the reset email, against a fake mail server), `test/server.test.mjs` and `test/browser.test.mjs` (see below). The server tests run against stand-ins for Claude, the news feeds and Twelve Data, so nothing real is called. They cover:

- **The rules:**
  - XP, streaks and freezes, badges and starting units;
  - money by country and the facts;
  - portfolio and goal math;
  - all 59 lessons, 31 Money Missions, every piece of app text and the glossary being complete in both languages and both countries;
  - Money Missions: who each fits, their order, the weekly suggestion, notes, XP once and achievements;
  - streak repair (2+ days, within 7 days, once a month) and days away.
- **The waitlist** (`test/waitlist.test.mjs`): the age rules, only the asked-for fields, the hidden bot field and rate limits, "already on the list", the confirmation email in both languages, the take-me-off link, the admin CSV (safe in spreadsheets) and delete.
- **Growth and schools** (`test/classroom.test.mjs`): the classroom switch; what a teacher needs (18+, confirmed email, terms); joining (13+, one nickname per class, closed classes, new codes, wrong-code throttling); what the device reports and what the teacher sees (never goals, amounts, notes, tutor questions, age or email; topic totals only from 3+ students); the class leaderboard and hiding; leaving and removing; paused, archived and deleted classes; invite rewards (once per friend, 20 at most) and friends; the recap's pause note and mailing address.
- **Reminders and the recap** (`test/retention.test.mjs`): encryption checked against RFC 8291's example, the VAPID signature, what each reminder says and when (time zone, quiet hours, once a day), push services only; the recap's content in both languages, Sunday evening once a week, stopping after 4 quiet weeks, the turn-off link.
- **The server:**
  - accounts, with the age and guardian rules;
  - progress sync and leagues (with the XP cap);
  - the tutor: server-side prompt, limits and monthly budget, and goal facts without goal names;
  - news, including dropping steered summaries;
  - prices;
  - analytics and retention math (day 1, 7 and 30, and what brings people back), quit points, the survey, feedback and errors, behind the admin token;
  - reminder sign-up (push services only, deleted when off) and the recap opt-in;
  - password reset (the same answer for any email, a link that works once, other sessions signed out);
  - deleting data and accounts.

`node scripts/tutor-check.mjs --yes` checks the real AI's answers. It costs money, so it only runs when you ask for it.
