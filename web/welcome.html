How it works

A few minutes a day, on your phone.

1

Set a goal

A car, college, an emergency fund or your first investment. You get a monthly plan and see whether you're on track.

2

Learn in four minutes

63 short lessons in 18 units: budgeting, your first paycheck, credit, scams, taxes, investing basics and more. Reviews bring back what you missed.

3

Practice with pretend money

A practice portfolio with $10,000 (or $200,000 pesos) of pretend money. More choices unlock as you learn.

4

Ask, then try it for real

An AI tutor explains terms and headlines in plain words, and never tells you what to buy. Real-life missions are small real-life tasks, like finding the fees on a bank account.

Who it's for

Teens, 13–17

Learn the basics before the money gets real. Making an account needs a parent's or guardian's OK, and teens only compete with other teens.

Young adults

First paychecks, first credit card, first investments — without the jargon.

Parents

A calm way for your teen to learn about money: no real money, no ads, nothing to buy.

Teachers and clubs

A classroom mode is coming. See below.

What it isn't

  • Not a bank or a broker. There's no real money, no real trades and no bank connections.
  • Not advice. Lessons explain how money works. They never tell you what to buy or sell.
  • No ads, and we never sell your data. Accounts are optional: without one, your progress stays on your phone.
  • Not finished. It's a small beta, so things will change. There's a feedback button on every screen, and we read everything.

Find your open water

Out on open water, you choose where you go. Nobody else sets your course, and that's about as free as a person gets. But you can't sail far until your money is under control: knowing where it goes, keeping some of it, and making it grow. That's what Northvale is for. Find your open water.

Our promise: no ads, we never sell your data, and nobody pays us to recommend anything.
