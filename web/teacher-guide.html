Northvale LEARN

Teacher guide

Using Northvale Learn with a class or a club. Short lessons, a practice portfolio and real-life missions for ages 13 and up — with pretend money only.

Classroom mode is a test. It's switched on only for invited teachers while a lawyer reviews it. Before you use it with students, get your school's OK and read the teacher terms (in the app: Classes → Teacher terms).

What Northvale Learn is

  • Four-minute lessons in 17 units, from budgeting and first paychecks to scams, credit, investing basics and taxes — in English or Spanish, for the US or Mexico.
  • Practice tools: a goal planner, calculators, and a practice portfolio with pretend money.
  • Money Missions: small real-life tasks ("find the fees on a bank account") that students do on their own.
  • Educational only: no real money, no bank connections, and nothing in it is financial advice.

Set up a class (about 5 minutes)

  1. Make an account in the app (Settings), for an adult 18 or older.
  2. Open Classes: confirm your email (we send a link) and accept the teacher terms.
  3. Create a class ("Period 3 Economics", "Money Club").
  4. Share the class code (project it) or the join link (post it where your class finds links).
  5. When everyone's in, turn off Taking new students. You can make a new code any time; the old one stops working.

What students do

  • They open the link (or enter the code) — no account or email needed.
  • Before joining, they see a screen that says exactly what you will and won't see.
  • They pick a nickname. Ask them not to use their full name.
  • They see your assignments with due dates, and a class leaderboard (nicknames and this week's XP). Anyone can hide from the leaderboard.
  • They can leave any time. Then you no longer see anything about them.
You see
  • Nicknames
  • Lessons and units finished since each student joined
  • How many Money Missions they finished
  • Progress on what you assign
  • Topics the class finds hard, as class totals (once 3+ students have answered)
You never see
  • Goals or any amounts
  • Practice portfolios
  • Mission notes
  • Questions to the AI tutor
  • Age or email
  • Anything from before they joined

There's no messaging of any kind — not between students, and not between you and students.

In a club or after-school program

  • Start each meeting with one lesson on the projector and vote on the answers.
  • Use the weekly class leaderboard for friendly competition — remind members they can hide.
  • Money Missions make good "homework" between meetings; students keep their notes private.

Privacy, in short

  • Students join with a nickname. A class never asks for names or emails — only an age range, to check they're 13 or older.
  • A student's class data is deleted when they leave or you remove them.
  • Archive a class at the end of the term: students can't use it anymore, and it's deleted 90 days later. Deleting your account deletes your classes.
  • Student information is never sold or used for ads.

Questions

Do students need phones? Any device with a web browser works: a phone, a school laptop or a tablet.
Can I message students? No. Northvale Learn has no messaging, on purpose.
A student lost their device. They join again with the code and a new nickname; remove the old row.
Is it free? Yes, during the test.
