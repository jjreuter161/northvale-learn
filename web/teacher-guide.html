Northvale LEARN

Teacher guide

Using Northvale Learn with a class or a club. Short lessons, a practice portfolio and real-life missions for ages 13 and up — with pretend money only.

Classroom mode is a free pilot. It's switched on only for invited teachers while a lawyer reviews it. Before you use it with students, get your school's OK and read the teacher terms (you agree to them when you set up your teacher account). No ads, ever, and student data is never sold or shared.

What Northvale Learn is

  • Four-minute lessons in 18 units, from budgeting and first paychecks to scams, credit, investing basics and taxes — in English or Spanish, for the US or Mexico.
  • Practice tools: a goal planner, calculators, and a practice portfolio with pretend money.
  • Real-life missions: small tasks ("find the fees on a bank account") that students do on their own.
  • Educational only: no real money, no bank connections, and nothing in it is financial advice.

Set up a class (about 5 minutes)

  1. Open the link Northvale sent you and choose I'm a teacher. One screen sets up your account: your email, a password, your age (teachers are adults) and the teacher terms.
  2. Confirm your email (we send a link).
  3. Name your class ("Period 3 Economics", "Money Club"). It opens with its code.
  4. Share the class code (project it) or the join link (post it where your class finds links).
  5. When everyone's in, open Class settings and turn off Taking new students. You can make a new code any time; the old one stops working.

What students do

  • They open the link, or tap Join a class and type the code — no account or email needed.
  • Before joining, they see a short screen that says exactly what you will and won't see.
  • They pick a nickname. Ask them not to use their full name.
  • They do the lessons you ask for. There are no assignments, due dates or leaderboards in the pilot: you tell the class which unit to do.
  • With a free account (a parent's or guardian's OK if they're under 18), their progress and the class follow them between school and home.
  • They can leave any time and keep their own progress. Then you no longer see anything about them.
You see
  • Nicknames
  • Which lessons each student finished since joining
  • For the pilot quiz and the end-of-pilot questions: class totals only, once 3 or more students have answered
You never see
  • Goals or any amounts
  • Practice portfolios
  • Mission notes
  • Questions to the AI tutor
  • Age or email
  • A student's own quiz score, or what they type at the end of the pilot
  • Anything from before they joined

There's no messaging of any kind — not between students, and not between you and students.

A four-week pilot (one class period a week, plus a few minutes at home)

Before week 1, open your class and pick the pilot's units under Pilot quiz (for example Budgeting, Saving and Banking for real). The before quiz and the after quiz use the same questions, so the difference shows what the class learned. You see totals only.

In a club or after-school program

  • Start each meeting with one lesson on the projector and vote on the answers.
  • Use the pilot quiz at the first and last meeting to see what the group learned (totals only).
  • Real-life missions make good "homework" between meetings; students keep their notes private.

Privacy, in short

  • Students join with a nickname. A class never asks for names or emails — only an age range, to check they're 13 or older.
  • A student's class data (finished lessons, quiz and pilot answers) is deleted when they leave or you remove them. Their own progress stays theirs.
  • End the class at the end of the term (Class settings): students can't use it anymore, and it's deleted 90 days later. Deleting your account deletes your classes.
  • Free during the pilot. No ads, ever. Student information is never sold or shared.

Questions

Do students need phones? Any device with a web browser works: a phone, a school laptop or a tablet.
Can I message students? No. Northvale Learn has no messaging, on purpose.
A student lost their device. With an account, they sign in on the new one and their class comes with them. Without one, they join again with the code and a new nickname; remove the old entry in Class settings.
Is it free? Yes, during the pilot. No ads.
